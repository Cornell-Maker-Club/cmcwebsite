Training

To gain access to the lab space and club resources, members need to attend an orientation session covering basic lab safety and rules. We offer machine-specific training to current members for our 3D printers, laser cutters, and will add wood-working tools and sewing machine trainings in the future.

Sign up for a session

Pick a session with open spots and enter your name, NetID and CUID (the 7-digit number on your Cornell ID card). You’ll get a confirmation at your Cornell email.

Each session has a limited number of spots. If you can no longer make it, tell the trainer so someone else can take your place.
