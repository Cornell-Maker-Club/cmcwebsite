Make‑a‑thon

Our annual 48-hour build event. Participants design and build projects in a space made for entrepreneurship and innovation.

48 hours To design, build and demo a project from scratch.
Equipment Access to the club’s prototyping equipment and supplies.
Prizes Awarded to the winning projects. Past prizes include 3D printers, VR headsets and tools.
Food & merch Two lunches, snacks and Make-a-thon merch for every participant.

The next Make-a-thon

Dates for the next Make-a-thon are announced on Instagram and the Maker Club Slack. Follow along so you don’t miss sign-ups.
