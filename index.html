Cornell’s Premier Space for Makers

The Cornell Maker Club (CMC) is a student-run club for experimenting with innovative technologies. We provide tools, workspace, and a community for all interested members of Cornell’s campus. Our purpose is to provide a space where motivated and creative minds can give shape to their ideas and to help spread this passion through the campus and beyond.

David Ahl & Hui Family Maker Lab

Our lab space houses a laser cutter, 3D printers, drill press, tabletop saw, adhesives, and a wide assortment of extra hand tools. It also features power supplies, signal generators, oscilloscopes, hand tools, drills, and a medley of electrical components, sensors, and actuators.

209 Phillips Hall, Cornell University, Ithaca, NY 14853

Notice

Cornell Maker Club resources are for personal projects only. They are not to be used for classes, research, or project teams. For those categories, please talk with your professor/advisor/team leads or visit the Rapid Prototyping Lab located in Upson Hall.

How to Join

Membership is open to everyone at Cornell: undergraduate and graduate students, and professors too. Members typically become engaged in one or more personal or group projects.

Training sessions have a limited number of slots and fill up quickly, so sign up early.

Join the SlackSign up for training
  1. 01 Join the Slack! This is where most of our communication goes out: training times, events and lab news. Join the Maker Club Slack.
  2. 02 Sign up for general training to get door access. Find open sessions on the Join page.
  3. 03 Within two weeks of your training, you should have card access to our lab space.
  4. 04 Want to use specific equipment like the 3D printers or laser cutter? Attend an additional training for that machine. Those sessions are on the Join page too.
