Online PCB Seminar

Learn to design, manufacture and test printed circuit boards. These notes and videos come from the 2020 seminar run by Katie Bradford.

About the seminar

The seminar combined presentations and videos on PCB design with a hands-on part: designing your own board, then assembling and testing it. CMC no longer provides the boards and components, but the notes and videos are still a great way to get started with PCB design.

Correction

One video uses the IPC-compliant footprint wizard’s SOJ option. Don’t use it: that’s a surface-mount package. Use the regular DIP footprint wizard instead, or make the footprint by hand.
