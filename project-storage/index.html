Project Storage

Keep your work safe between lab visits, and keep the workspace clear for everyone else.

Where to store projects

Shelves
D1 and D2 in the Maker Lab, 209 Phillips Hall
Boxes
Standard white project boxes, marked with your name and NetID

Project need more space than a box? Reach out to any board member.

Storage rules

  1. 01 Store projects in a standard white box on the designated storage shelves, D1 and D2. If your project needs more space, reach out to any board member.
  2. 02 Mark every box with your name and NetID.
  3. 03 Register your project each semester to confirm you’re still working on it. Unregistered projects risk being discarded.
  4. 04 Projects left outside the boxes without permission will be discarded.
  5. 05 Projects left on the workbenches for an extended period of time will be discarded.
